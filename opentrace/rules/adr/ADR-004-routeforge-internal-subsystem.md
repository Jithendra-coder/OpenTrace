# ADR-004: RouteForge Is an Internal Subsystem

## Status
Accepted.
## Context
Routing depends on ChangeMesh impact and migration features.
## Decision
Keep RouteForge internal and start no earlier than M11.
## Alternatives Considered
Separate initial product; early router.
## Consequences
No premature routing claims; shared typed contracts.
## Revisit Conditions
Product evidence demonstrates an independent boundary and user need.
