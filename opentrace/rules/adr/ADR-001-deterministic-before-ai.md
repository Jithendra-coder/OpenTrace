# ADR-001: Deterministic Before AI

## Status
Accepted.
## Context
Contract and static evidence must be reliable before generative repair.
## Decision
Build deterministic analysis, migration where applicable, and validation before AI generation.
## Alternatives Considered
LLM-first migration; parallel AI implementation.
## Consequences
Slower apparent feature breadth, stronger explainability and baselines.
## Revisit Conditions
Only to expand proven deterministic/AI boundaries with measured evidence.
