# RouteForge Plan

RouteForge is an internal OpenTrace subsystem, beginning at M11 and no earlier, after meaningful impact and migration-complexity features exist. It estimates `P(validated repair succeeds | migration features, repair strategy)` for `NO_AI`, `DETERMINISTIC`, `SMALL`, `MEDIUM`, and `STRONG`. `NO_AI` means no generated repair is required; `DETERMINISTIC` means a programmatic candidate patch is produced. It is not a generic LLM router detached from migration analysis.

Policies are Economy, Balanced, and Critical. Optimize minimum expected cost subject to predicted validation success meeting a policy threshold and expected latency meeting an SLA. Compare Always Small, Always Strong, Random, Rule Based, Logistic Regression, and XGBoost; the primary comparison is RouteForge versus Always Strong. Record validated repair success, average cost, cost per successful repair, average/p95 latency, constraint violations, strong-model usage, escalation rate and attempts to success. Contextual bandits, token-budget routing, and online learning are advanced work, not prerequisites. Record estimate uncertainty; deterministic/AI-disabled fallbacks remain functional. No cost-savings claim is allowed without measured cost.
